About Prompt Kit PH

A free library of original prompt kits and guides, made for first time and busy creators.

What this site is

Each kit is a web page with a prompt framework, examples, tips to adapt it and a fact check checklist. You can print any kit or save it as a PDF from your browser.

How the content is made

Kits and guides are written for this site and drafted with AI assistance, then reviewed. They do not contain verified facts about their topics. The prompts ask you to check facts yourself before publishing.

AI-assisted content. Check facts before you publish.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no sign ups.
  • No analytics, ads or trackers.
  • No collection of what you type in the builder.

Read the privacy page for details.
