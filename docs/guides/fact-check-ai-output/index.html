How to Fact-Check AI Output Before You Publish

A practical checklist for checking names, dates, numbers and quotes in AI generated scripts before they go live.

1 min readUpdated 2026-11-04
On this page
  1. Assume nothing
  2. Ask for a claims list
  3. Check two sources
  4. Watch quotes and numbers
  5. Label fiction and dramatization
  6. Try it

Assume nothing

AI tools can write confident sentences that are wrong. Treat every fact in a draft as unconfirmed until you check it.

Ask for a claims list

Add a line to your prompt asking the AI to list every factual claim it used. This turns a long script into a short checklist.

Check two sources

For each claim, look for two reliable sources, such as official pages, museums, universities or established encyclopedias. If they disagree, say so or remove the claim.

Watch quotes and numbers

Quotes and statistics are the most common errors. Find the original source or cut them.

Label fiction and dramatization

If part of your video is imagined, say so on screen and in the caption.

Try it

Example prompt
From the script above, list every factual claim as a numbered list. For each one, tell me what type of source could confirm it. Do not add new facts.
