JEEPNEY HISTORY EXPLAINER KIT
Prompt Kit PH | Philippines kit

OVERVIEW
Tell the story of the Philippine jeepney: its design, its art and its role in daily commutes, with careful dates.
The jeepney is a strong local topic. Its history is often told with confident dates that are worth checking, so this kit builds the check in.

PROMPT FRAMEWORK (replace anything in square brackets)

01 ROLE
You are a Filipino culture storyteller who explains local history with pride and care.

02 CONTEXT
Topic: the history and design of the jeepney. Audience: young Filipino viewers and visitors. Length: about 90 seconds. Use [YEAR?] wherever a date needs checking. Include a scene on the art and decorations.

03 OUTPUT
Give me: 1) a 7 scene script, 2) one image prompt per scene in a colorful poster style, 3) a list of dates and claims to verify with local sources.

FULL PROMPT (copy and paste this)
You are a Filipino culture storyteller who explains local history with pride and care.
Topic: the history and design of the jeepney. Audience: young Filipino viewers and visitors. Length: about 90 seconds. Use [YEAR?] wherever a date needs checking. Include a scene on the art and decorations.
Give me: 1) a 7 scene script, 2) one image prompt per scene in a colorful poster style, 3) a list of dates and claims to verify with local sources.

EXAMPLE PROMPTS

Scene image prompt
Colorful poster style illustration of a decorated jeepney on a busy city street at golden hour, chrome details and painted patterns, original design, no real brand names.

Art scene prompt
Describe five decorative details often seen on jeepneys in simple words so I can use them as image prompt ideas.

HOW TO ADAPT IT
1. Focus on one city's routes or on the art alone.
2. Make a 'then and now' comparison, checking current transport policy with official sources.
3. Interview a driver and add their own words.

FACT CHECK CHECKLIST
- Check dates with the National Historical Commission of the Philippines, museums or reliable news archives.
- Check any statement about current transport rules with official government pages.
- Compare every date, name and number against at least two reliable sources, such as an official site, a museum, a university page or a respected encyclopedia.
