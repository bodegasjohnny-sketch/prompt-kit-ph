UNUSUAL JOBS EXPLAINER KIT
Prompt Kit PH | Careers kit

OVERVIEW
Spotlight a rare, tough or surprising job with respect for the people who do it.
Job stories can slip into stereotypes or dramatic claims about pay and danger. This kit focuses on the work itself and asks you to confirm details.

PROMPT FRAMEWORK (replace anything in square brackets)

01 ROLE
You are a respectful documentary narrator who explains what a job involves day to day.

02 CONTEXT
Topic: the job of a [JOB]. Audience: students exploring careers. Length: about 60 seconds. Do not state salaries or injury figures unless I provide a source.

03 OUTPUT
Give me: 1) a 'day in the life' script in 6 scenes, 2) image prompts in a documentary illustration style, 3) questions I could ask a real worker to confirm the details.

FULL PROMPT (copy and paste this)
You are a respectful documentary narrator who explains what a job involves day to day.
Topic: the job of a [JOB]. Audience: students exploring careers. Length: about 60 seconds. Do not state salaries or injury figures unless I provide a source.
Give me: 1) a 'day in the life' script in 6 scenes, 2) image prompts in a documentary illustration style, 3) questions I could ask a real worker to confirm the details.

EXAMPLE PROMPTS

Scene image prompt
Documentary style illustration of a worker in safety gear at dawn preparing equipment, respectful portrait, muted navy tones with a red accent, no logos.

Interview prompt
Write eight polite interview questions for a [JOB] about their daily routine, skills and safety practices.

HOW TO ADAPT IT
1. Pick a job in your own town and interview someone who does it.
2. Turn the script into a 'skills you need' carousel.
3. Add a closing scene about how to learn more safely.

FACT CHECK CHECKLIST
- Confirm details with a real worker, a trade association or an official labor page.
- Avoid salary and danger statistics unless sourced.
- Read the script out loud once. Cut anything that sounds like a guess stated as a fact.
