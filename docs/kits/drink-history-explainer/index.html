Overview

Create a responsible history explainer about a traditional drink, its region and how it is made, without promoting drinking.

Drink history mixes culture, trade and craft. The prompt keeps the tone educational and adds an age reminder for alcoholic drinks.

Prompt framework

Copy each part, or copy the full prompt. Replace anything in square brackets.

01 Role

Role
You are a food and culture historian who explains traditions respectfully and never encourages drinking.

02 Context

Context
Topic: the history of [DRINK]. Audience: adults interested in culture. Length: about 90 seconds. If the drink contains alcohol, add a short line that it is for adults of legal drinking age only. Flag legends separately from documented history.

03 Output

Output
Give me: 1) a 7 scene script, 2) one image prompt per scene in a vintage engraving style, 3) a separate list of legends versus documented facts for me to verify.

Full prompt

Full prompt
You are a food and culture historian who explains traditions respectfully and never encourages drinking.
Topic: the history of [DRINK]. Audience: adults interested in culture. Length: about 90 seconds. If the drink contains alcohol, add a short line that it is for adults of legal drinking age only. Flag legends separately from documented history.
Give me: 1) a 7 scene script, 2) one image prompt per scene in a vintage engraving style, 3) a separate list of legends versus documented facts for me to verify.

Scene image prompt

Scene image prompt

Legend check prompt

Legend check prompt
From the script you wrote, list every story that might be a legend. For each, tell me what kind of source would confirm or reject it.

How to adapt it

  1. Try a non alcoholic drink such as a traditional tea or chocolate drink.
  2. Focus on one region instead of the whole history.
  3. Turn it into a 'how it is made' explainer with process steps.

More tips in how to adapt a kit to your project.

Fact check checklist

  • Keep the legal age reminder if the drink contains alcohol.
  • Separate legends from history in the final script.
  • Compare every date, name and number against at least two reliable sources, such as an official site, a museum, a university page or a respected encyclopedia.
  • Remove any claim you could not confirm. If you keep a disputed point, label it as disputed.
