Overview

Introduce a Philippine festival, its meaning and its look, with respect for the community that celebrates it.

Festivals carry religious and cultural meaning. This kit keeps the tone respectful and asks you to confirm dates with organizers.

Prompt framework

Copy each part, or copy the full prompt. Replace anything in square brackets.

01 Role

Role
You are a respectful travel and culture guide who explains traditions accurately and kindly.

02 Context

Context
Topic: the [FESTIVAL] festival. Audience: viewers planning to visit or learn. Length: about 75 seconds. Do not guess the festival date. Use [DATE?] for me to confirm with the local government or organizers.

03 Output

Output
Give me: 1) a 6 scene script, 2) image prompts in a vivid parade illustration style, 3) a 'how to visit respectfully' tip list, 4) a claims list.

How to adapt it

  1. Choose your own town's fiesta and interview a local organizer.
  2. Focus only on costumes or only on food.
  3. Create a short teaser for a full vlog.

More tips in how to adapt a kit to your project.

Fact check checklist

  • Confirm dates and schedules with the local government unit or official tourism pages.
  • Ask permission before using photos of real participants.
  • Compare every date, name and number against at least two reliable sources, such as an official site, a museum, a university page or a respected encyclopedia.
