COSMIC ORIGIN MAP KIT
Prompt Kit PH | Space kit

OVERVIEW
Explain the origin of a planet, moon or star system with glowing space map visuals and careful science wording.
Space science changes as new research comes in. This kit asks for current consensus wording and marks theories as theories.

PROMPT FRAMEWORK (replace anything in square brackets)

01 ROLE
You are an astronomy educator who explains space science clearly and separates theories from confirmed observations.

02 CONTEXT
Topic: how [OBJECT] formed. Audience: curious students. Length: about 90 seconds. Use phrases like 'scientists think' for theories. List claims for me to check with a space agency source.

03 OUTPUT
Give me: 1) a 6 scene script that moves from the early cloud of gas to today, 2) one image prompt per scene in a glowing space map style, 3) a claims list.

FULL PROMPT (copy and paste this)
You are an astronomy educator who explains space science clearly and separates theories from confirmed observations.
Topic: how [OBJECT] formed. Audience: curious students. Length: about 90 seconds. Use phrases like 'scientists think' for theories. List claims for me to check with a space agency source.
Give me: 1) a 6 scene script that moves from the early cloud of gas to today, 2) one image prompt per scene in a glowing space map style, 3) a claims list.

EXAMPLE PROMPTS

Space map prompt
Glowing space map illustration showing a swirling disk of dust around a young star, deep navy background, soft red and white highlights, no text.

Theory check prompt
For each scene in the script, say whether it describes an observation, a widely accepted theory or an open question.

HOW TO ADAPT IT
1. Swap the object: the Moon, Saturn's rings, a comet.
2. Make a 'scale' version comparing sizes, then check every size.
3. Shorten to a 30 second teaser.

FACT CHECK CHECKLIST
- Check claims with official space agency or observatory pages.
- Keep 'scientists think' wording for theories.
- Remove any claim you could not confirm. If you keep a disputed point, label it as disputed.
