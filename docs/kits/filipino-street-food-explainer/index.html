FILIPINO STREET FOOD EXPLAINER KIT
Open Source Prompt PH | Philippines kit

OVERVIEW
Explain a Filipino street food: how it is made, where you find it and food safety tips, in a bright and friendly style.
Street food videos are fun and shareable. This kit adds a food safety note and avoids claims about origin that you cannot confirm.

PROMPT FRAMEWORK (replace anything in square brackets)

01 ROLE
You are a cheerful food guide who loves Filipino street food and cares about food safety.

02 CONTEXT
Topic: [STREET FOOD]. Audience: local viewers and visitors. Length: about 60 seconds. Describe taste and preparation. Mark any origin story for checking.

03 OUTPUT
Give me: 1) a 6 scene script, 2) one image prompt per scene in a warm food illustration style, 3) one food safety tip, 4) a claims list.

FULL PROMPT (copy and paste this)
You are a cheerful food guide who loves Filipino street food and cares about food safety.
Topic: [STREET FOOD]. Audience: local viewers and visitors. Length: about 60 seconds. Describe taste and preparation. Mark any origin story for checking.
Give me: 1) a 6 scene script, 2) one image prompt per scene in a warm food illustration style, 3) one food safety tip, 4) a claims list.

EXAMPLE PROMPTS

Scene image prompt
Warm illustration of a street food cart at night with skewers on a grill, soft smoke and string lights, friendly vendor, no text or logos.

Taste words prompt
Give me ten simple words to describe the taste and texture of [STREET FOOD] for a short caption.

HOW TO ADAPT IT
1. Swap the dish: fish balls, kwek kwek, turon, taho.
2. Compare two dishes in a 'which one are you' format.
3. Make it a recipe style video and test the recipe yourself.

FACT CHECK CHECKLIST
- Check food safety tips with official health department guidance.
- Mark origin stories as 'often said' unless you find a reliable source.
- Read the script out loud once. Cut anything that sounds like a guess stated as a fact.
