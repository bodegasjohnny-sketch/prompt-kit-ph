Overview

Tell the story of a distant dwarf planet, like Eris, Pluto or Ceres, from discovery to what is still unknown.

Small far away worlds have fewer confirmed facts, which makes careful wording even more important. This kit leans into the mystery honestly.

Prompt framework

Copy each part, or copy the full prompt. Replace anything in square brackets.

01 Role

Role
You are a space storyteller who is honest about what scientists do not know yet.

02 Context

Context
Topic: the dwarf planet [NAME]. Audience: general viewers. Length: about 75 seconds. Include a scene about open questions. Mark every number for verification.

03 Output

Output
Give me: 1) a 5 scene script, 2) one space map image prompt per scene, 3) a list of numbers and dates to check.

Full prompt

Full prompt
You are a space storyteller who is honest about what scientists do not know yet.
Topic: the dwarf planet [NAME]. Audience: general viewers. Length: about 75 seconds. Include a scene about open questions. Mark every number for verification.

Open questions prompt

How to adapt it

  1. Compare two dwarf planets side by side.
  2. Tell it from the point of view of a discovery team, labeled as dramatized.
  3. Add an orbit diagram scene.

More tips in how to adapt a kit to your project.

Fact check checklist

  • Confirm discovery dates and sizes with an official space agency page.
  • Label dramatized scenes as dramatized.
  • Compare every date, name and number against at least two reliable sources, such as an official site, a museum, a university page or a respected encyclopedia.
