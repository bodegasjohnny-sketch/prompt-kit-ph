 THE ERIS ORIGIN MAP EXPLAINER
 (A) Title + topic line. Cosmic Origin Map Explainer Reels Automation | V9.3 order | High-Retention Engine | 11 scenes x 7 seconds =
 77 seconds | 9:16 animated 3D cosmic-map documentary.
 Topic: Eris (136199 Eris), dwarf planet in the scattered disc. Question the video answers: who discovered it, from where and when,
 who named it and why, and what is the real story? Narration language: English (default). Hero object: Eris floats highlighted at the
 center of every cut of all 11 scenes. Naming tale (labeled as a tale): the belief that "Xena" was its official name. Documented record:
 the discoverers proposed Eris and the IAU approved it in September 2006. Data taken from the uploaded Cosmic Origin Map Explainer
 PDF (structure, rules, hero-object lock, Data Sheet format) and the reference links openai/openai-realtime-solar-system,
 Ishanoshada/Planet3D, github.com/topics/solar-system-simulation and y-bash/SolarSystemSimulator (used as render-route
 references).

Overview

Tell the story of a distant dwarf planet, like Eris, Pluto or Ceres, from discovery to what is still unknown.

Small far away worlds have fewer confirmed facts, which makes careful wording even more important. This kit leans into the mystery honestly.
