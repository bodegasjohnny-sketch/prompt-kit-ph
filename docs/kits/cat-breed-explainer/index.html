Overview

Introduce one cat breed with a gentle narrated script, matching illustrations and a short care checklist.

Cat content does well when it feels cozy and accurate. The framework separates personality notes from facts you must confirm.

Prompt framework

Copy each part, or copy the full prompt. Replace anything in square brackets.

01 Role

Role
You are a patient cat care writer who avoids exaggeration and explains things for beginners.

02 Context

Context
Topic: the [BREED NAME]. Audience: people living in small condos or apartments. Length: about 60 seconds. Tell me which statements are general observations and which need a source.

03 Output

Output
Give me: 1) a hook line, 2) a 5 scene script, 3) one image prompt per scene, 4) a short care checklist, 5) a list of claims to verify.

Full prompt

Full prompt
You are a patient cat care writer who avoids exaggeration and explains things for beginners.
Topic: the [BREED NAME]. Audience: people living in small condos or apartments. Length: about 60 seconds. Tell me which statements are general observations and which need a source.
Give me: 1) a hook line, 2) a 5 scene script, 3) one image prompt per scene, 4) a short care checklist, 5) a list of claims to verify.

Example prompts

These are original examples written for this kit. Results vary by tool, so review every output.

Scene image prompt

Scene image prompt
Cozy illustration of a [BREED NAME] cat sitting by a condo window with a city skyline behind it, soft pastel colors, calm mood, no text or logos.

Hook prompt

Hook prompt
Write five opening hooks for a short video about the [BREED NAME]. Keep each under 12 words. No claims about rarity or price.

How to adapt it

  1. Change the setting from a condo to a house with a garden.
  2. Replace the care checklist with a 'questions to ask a breeder or shelter' list.
  3. Turn the script into a 4 slide carousel by merging scenes.

More tips in how to adapt a kit to your project.

Fact check checklist

  • Confirm care and diet advice with a licensed veterinarian.
  • Compare every date, name and number against at least two reliable sources, such as an official site, a museum, a university page or a respected encyclopedia.
  • Remove any claim you could not confirm. If you keep a disputed point, label it as disputed.
