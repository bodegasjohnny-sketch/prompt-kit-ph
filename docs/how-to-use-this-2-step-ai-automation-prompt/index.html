From Free Prompt to Finished Video

START HERE · THREE SHORT VIDEO GUIDES

New to the free prompt kits? Follow these three stages: prepare your prompt, edit your generated assets, and review a finished example. Pause or replay each video at your own pace.

Your workflow at a glance

01 — Use the prompt → 02 — Edit your video → 03 — See the result

You will need your chosen prompt kit, an AI tool that accepts its instructions, a video editor and a place to save your assets. External tools may require their own paid plans.

STEP 01 · 1 min 25 sec

Use your free prompt

Start with a topic. Build a clear creative brief.

This walkthrough shows a prompt framework in ChatGPT, followed by the generated title, narration, audio plan, scene prompts and thumbnail section.

  1. Choose a free niche or creator kit from the website and open its prompt or PDF.
  2. Upload the PDF to a compatible AI tool, or paste the prompt text. Replace any topic fields with your chosen subject.
  3. Read the instructions in that kit and review the generated title, narration and scene-by-scene plan.
  4. Copy each scene’s image or video prompt into your chosen generation tool. Review the results before taking them into your editor.

Keep the script and scene prompts in one document so you can match each visual to the right narration.

STEP 02 · 1 min 05 sec

Edit your AI video

Turn separate assets into a finished story.

This screen recording shows a video-editing timeline with multiple visual clips, audio tracks and text elements. Use it as a visual reference for arranging your own project.

  1. Import your generated visuals and narration into your video editor.
  2. Arrange the clips in the same order as your script, then trim each clip to fit the narration.
  3. Add readable captions and any music or sound effects you have permission to use. Keep speech clear above background audio.
  4. Watch the full timeline before exporting. Check the beginning, scene transitions, text placement and ending.

Use the video’s fullscreen control to inspect the editing timeline more closely.

STEP 03 · 1 min 03 sec

See a finished video example

See how the pieces work together.

Watch a completed vertical documentary-style example with animated maps, close-up visuals and on-screen text. This is a format example, not a promise of identical results.

  1. Notice how the opening introduces the subject visually.
  2. Watch how the visuals change as the story progresses.
  3. Check how text is positioned and how the scenes fit the narration.
  4. Apply the structure to your own subject, then verify factual claims before publishing.

The prompt walkthrough and finished example use different topics. They demonstrate stages of the workflow rather than one identical project from start to finish.

Before you publish

  • Confirm names, dates and other factual claims against reliable sources. AI output can be wrong.
  • Check that your images, music and other assets are permitted for your intended use.
  • Preview your video on a phone. Make captions readable and keep important text away from interface overlays.
  • Review sound levels and remove accidental private information from screen recordings.
  • Make your own editorial choices: revise the script, improve unclear scenes and add context your viewers need.

The written steps are a companion guide, not a verbatim transcript. Results vary with your topic, tools and editing decisions.
