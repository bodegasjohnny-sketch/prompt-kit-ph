- ACT 2: "For decades, every [demonym] [learner] memorized one number: [OLD] [units], [where it was

Voice rules: third person except the single YOU line; present or simple past; declarative, documentary. Every scene
is exactly 15-17 words (count whitespace-separated tokens; rewrite any scene outside range). Numbers as digits

method once, in Act 5 only. Keep named incidents (TV moments, quotes, people) out of the script unless verified
and well documented.

MICRO-PACING & THE "YOU" TRIGGER
Sentences must cross cuts. Word-reveal cadence: 0.25-0.55 s per word. Every 6.0 s scene holds a minimum of 4
visual events: three cuts or camera beats (about 1.5-2.0 s each) plus one sub-second flash (0.3-0.5 s) on the key
number or key word at the scene midpoint. No static frame longer than 2.0 s. The counter card animates (tick, flip or
glow) on every scene change.
YOU_TRIGGER=ON (default for this edition): insert exactly ONE second-person line, 40-70 characters, in Act 6 (or
Act 4-5 if the narration fits better), tied to a shared instinct or shared memory, never to feelings.
YOU_TRIGGER=OFF: no second-person words anywhere.

RETENTION ENGINE (HIGH-RETENTION LAYER)
- R1 - Frame-one hook: no logo, no intro, no greeting. Counter card and first caption word are on screen at 0.0 s;
   first spoken word lands by 0.3 s.
- R2 - Two open loops: Loop A opens in Act 1 ("not a single one is new") and closes in Acts 4-5. Loop B opens in
   Act 6 (gold ? on the counter, definition question) and closes in Act 7. Never close a loop in the scene that
   opened it.
- R3 - Re-hook every 6.0 s: each scene boundary must change at least two of: map style, camera scale, counter
   state, audio texture. Hard cuts (no dissolves) on method changes (modern map to parchment in Act 4;
   parchment to radar lens in Act 5).
- R4 - Negation beat in Act 3: rule out the obvious cause before giving the real one. This is the highest-value
   retention line in the script.
- R5 - Counter as spine: a viewer watching with sound off must still follow the story through the counter states
   alone.
- R6 - Loop seam: the final frame matches the first frame (same map framing, same counter layout); the last
   word's audio tail bridges into the first word so replays feel continuous.
- R7 - Comment bait: the Reels Description ends with one genuine question tied to Act 6 (e.g., a definition the
   audience will argue about). Never ask for likes or follows on screen or in voice.
- R8 - Anti-repetition: no word (other than numbers and the place name) appears more than twice in the full
   narration; no two scenes open with the same word; no caption card repeats an earlier card.

UNIFYING AUDITORY ESCALATION & VOICE DELIVERY
Audio bed: low sustained drone, flat at -15 to -19 dB RMS, rising a half-step in pitch each scene. One soft tick
synced to every counter change. Act 4 hard cut: 0.3 s near-silence, then paper/rustle texture. Act 5: rising
radar-sweep tone. Act 6: drone drops out, single water/tide sound. Act 7: drone resolves to one clean tone on the
final word. Voice: calm, documentary, confident, medium-low pitch, about 2.7 words/second. Emphasis only on
numbers and the Act 3 negation line. Pause cues: 0.4 s before the Act 6 question and before the Act 7 imperative.
