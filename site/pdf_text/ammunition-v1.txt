 Act 2 / Scene 2 / 6.0-12.0s: Primary Function. Show one broad function with a simple, readable explanatory animation.
 Act 3 / Scene 3 / 12.0-18.0s: Micro-architecture. Reveal one verified component or material relationship. Resolve part of the

 Act 4 / Scene 4 / 18.0-24.0s: Pivot. Introduce one supported contrast: history, category distinction, design tradeoff, or

 Act 7 / Scene 7 / 36.0-42.0s: Closing Reframe. Deliver the promised payoff and connect the final visual and phrase naturally
 to Act 1.

 ARC RULE - REDUCTION REVEAL (MANDATORY 3 BEATS)
 Beat A / Act 1: Present a common narrow view and the feature that makes the topic more interesting. Beat B / Act 4: Expand
 that view using a supported contrast. Beat C / Act 7: Reframe the topic using the facts already established. Do not force
 multiple identities, secondary functions, or exaggerated system-wide significance.

 RETENTION RULES
 One main fact per scene. Show the evidence while the relevant words are spoken. Maintain one primary curiosity gap and
 resolve it by Scene 7. Each new scene must advance the explanation. Alternate detail, context, and whole-object scale while
 preserving object identity. Let the viewer read essential labels before accelerating. Avoid repeating the same orbit, speed
 ramp, or sound impact in all scenes.

 SEAMLESS LOOP

 first sentence only if both first playback and repeated playback sound natural. The ending must provide a payoff before the

 SLOT GRAMMARS

 ending for a natural bridge into Act 1.
