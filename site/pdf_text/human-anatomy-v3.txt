4. DYNAMIC NARRATION ENGINE

Build narration from slot grammars, never fixed sentences. Fill every slot with topic-accurate content.

   ACT 1: "The [organ]. [Size/count/location descriptor], often reduced to [common narrow role], yet essential to [broad outcome]."
   ACT 2: "[They/It] [primary verb + product/output], [definition of that output], through a [adjective] process called [TERM], where
   [stage 1], [stage 2] and [stage 3]."
   ACT 3: "Inside each [organ] are [structure descriptor] called [TERM], where this [process] occurs. There, [cell/unit] [action] and
   gradually [outcome], supported by specialized [helper cells/structures] that help regulate [what]."
   ACT 4: "But the [organ] do more than [primary verb]. They are also the body's primary source of [secondary output], a [class] that
   influences [item 1], [item 2] and [item 3]. This [class] is [delivery route] and helps regulate functions throughout the body, far
   beyond [narrow role]."
   ACT 5: "The [organ] must [condition + degree], which is why they are [placement/structure]. This [condition] control is critical for
   [function]." (If no natural temperature constraint exists, substitute the strongest real physical dependency. Never invent a false
   dependency.)
   ACT 6: "They also respond to signals from the [controller], which help control both [A] and [B] through a tightly regulated system."
   ACT 7: "[Adj], [adj] and [adj], the [organ] are more than [narrow role] structures. They are [identity 1], [identity 2] and key
   regulators of [system]."
