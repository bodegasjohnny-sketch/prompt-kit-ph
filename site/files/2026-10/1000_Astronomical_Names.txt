Planets, dwarf planets, moons, stars, galaxies, and superclusters
Compiled: 31 October 2026

SCOPE
This is a selected list of exactly 1,000 entries, not all objects in the universe.
Catalog designations (such as NGC numbers) are valid astronomical identifiers,
but are not descriptive proper names. Alternative names in parentheses refer
to the same object and do not count as extra entries.

Planets and moons occur within star systems and galaxies. Galaxies occur in
groups and clusters, some of which form superclusters. This list does NOT assign
every object to a specific supercluster. Supercluster boundaries and definitions
vary between studies. The six large-scale structures listed at the end are
identified as watershed superclusters in the cited 2023 cosmography study.

Only the eight Solar System planets are listed; exoplanets are not included.
Dwarf planets are kept separate. Moons use named entries from the JPL table;
unnamed provisional satellites are omitted. The star selection uses a 2022
IAU catalog snapshot plus the Sun; it is not a complete current named-star list.
Galaxy entries are OpenNGC records classified as G (individual galaxy), excluding
star clusters, nebulae, galaxy-pair records, and catalog duplicate records.
